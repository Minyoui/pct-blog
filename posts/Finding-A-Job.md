Finding and landing a job in 2026 is quite challenging right now.

It's been months since I have graduated and I tried upskilling for most of the time. Alotting time for each day, browsing and applying to different job applications. The first part is easy but having or receiving a reply from different employers is very unlikely. Most of applications are being ghosted, though some do reply quickly and inform you about their decision for your application but receiving rejection is much more acceptable than being ghosted as you tend to always think of your application if you are indeed eligible or not for the role. 

I wish employers will have the time to at least spend a quick minute or even a second to send a quick reply to us who are applying and let us know if we are proceeding or not. 

A quick and simple message is much more acceptable than rather ignoring the application.
