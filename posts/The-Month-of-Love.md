Late Happy Valentine's Day!!

This month is just filled with love and surprises. I just recently celebrated my birthday last week and I have come up with tons of thoughts about self-love.

Being someone who is currently exploring a lot in life in terms of skills and experiences. Something that I always stumble upon is having an argument with myself. Whether it is about missed opportunities or not actually liking my personal image. Losing love to your self degrades your self-esteem a lot to the point that you will continuously judge your actions even before you do them. You lose faith on your potential and miss out on skills that you should've obtained and learned. Experiencing doubts and asking yourself "Can I even do it?"

These thoughts always haunt me and it lowers my self-esteem quite a lot. So I tried understanding myself and accepting who I am. I slowly realized that simply accepting and loving yourself can actually make you feel happy. 

That old stigma of mine that thinking love is only given and received to someone or from someone. But I lately learned that love can also be given to one's self. 

Always remember to be easy on yourself. You are loved by many and you should also love yourself! Have some care and joy to ourselves this month!
