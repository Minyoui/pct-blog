What I have learned on upskilling for the past few months is not to chase knowledge and success but rather enjoy and have fun along the way. Always thinking of "not being enough" or "this is too hard" is dragging me down on improving myself. 

I found out that having fun while learning is very rewarding. I try not to compare myself to others who have more skills than me as comparison is the greatest thief of happiness. I reward myself by allowing myself to relax or go out with my family.
